08
Payments Growth Wedge

Make Payments The Easiest Revenue Expansion Story

If billing, invoices, and ledgers already live in Rev.io, collecting payments through QuickBooks creates avoidable friction and cost.

Customer Message

  • Stop paying QuickBooks to collect Rev.io invoices.
  • Billing and payments belong together.
  • From invoice to payment without the sync headache.
  • Lower payment fees where Rev.io can credibly prove savings.

Assets Needed

  • Before/after workflow: Rev.io invoice to QuickBooks payment vs. Rev.io invoice to Rev.io payment.
  • Calculator: payment rate delta, reconciliation time, sync breakage risk.
  • FAQ for migration, reconciliation, and payment claim boundaries.

RMM And Security Guardrails

Do Not Lead With Security. Lead With Service Monetization.

Market Message

  • Rev.io is RMM/security agnostic.
  • Acronis: cloud backup first, then EDR, then RMM.
  • NinjaOne co-marketing supports RMM + Acronis backup/EDR + Rev.io service/billing workflows.
  • One bill, not three bills for three service types.

Guardrails

  • Connect alerts and endpoint work to ticketing, service workflow, invoicing, and payment.
  • Label integrated/partner-powered capabilities clearly.
  • Avoid “Rev.io is a security platform.”

Paid And Organic Distribution

Use Paid Channels To Build Intent Audiences, And Organic Social To Extend Every Asset

H2 distribution should prioritize channels already showing high click-through and audience-building potential, while tightening the landing-page and content engine needed to convert that attention.

Paid Search / Paid Social

  • Optimize branded and competitive Google Paid Search campaigns.
  • Experiment with targeted campaigns built around high-value, low-cost keyword clusters.
  • Prioritize video content on LinkedIn, Reddit, and X to quickly build retargeting audiences.
  • Prioritize competitive campaigns on LinkedIn and Reddit where click-through rates are strong.
  • Create more competitive landing pages based on battle cards to support paid social and search campaigns.
  • Monitor Meta ads to determine whether it remains viable for driving MSP leads.

Organic Social

  • Create a content distribution engine to automate reshares of marketing-generated content across Reddit, X, and LinkedIn.
  • Turn every case study, webinar, eBook, product page, and competitive asset into reusable social distribution.
  • Invest in software like Dripify to build followers for key executive stakeholders most active on LinkedIn.
  • Use executive audience growth to increase reach, trust, and retargeting pool quality.
