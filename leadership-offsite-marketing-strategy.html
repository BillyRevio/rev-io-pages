Payments as a growth wedge

15 min

Buyer problem

Many customers already have billing, invoices, and ledgers in Rev.io but still sync invoice data to QuickBooks to collect payment. That creates broken connections, extra reconciliation, higher rates, and fear around moving payment workflows.

Strategic message

If Rev.io already manages billing and invoices, payments are the logical next step. Remove the middleman, collect through Rev.io, reduce fees, and keep invoice-to-payment workflows connected.

Campaign angles

  • Stop paying QuickBooks to collect Rev.io invoices
  • Billing and payments belong together
  • From invoice to payment without the sync headache
  • Payments savings assessment

Proof to build

  • Payments savings calculator
  • QuickBooks replacement guide
  • Accounting/reconciliation FAQ
  • Customer education webinar

Campaign priorities

15 min

1. Platform narrative

Lead with service operations + revenue management while preserving PSA, telecom billing, payments, and quote-to-cash keyword capture.

2. Competitive engine

Launch competitor alternative pages, paid campaigns, and sales one-sheets for named displacement targets.

3. Payments wedge

Educate buyers on moving payment collection out of QuickBooks and into Rev.io.

4. Closed-lost revival

Segment by loss reason and competitor; nurture based on timing, missing capabilities, no-show, and incumbent pain.

5. Community proof

Use Brook/community to create trusted educational surface area and capture buyer objections from the field.

6. Attribution discipline

Tighten UTMs and source/sub-source tracking so paid and content decisions connect to Salesforce outcomes.
