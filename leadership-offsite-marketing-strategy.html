08
Payments Growth Wedge

Make Payments The Easiest Revenue Expansion Story

If billing, invoices, and ledgers already live in Rev.io, collecting payments through QuickBooks creates avoidable friction and cost.

Working Copy

  • Stop paying QuickBooks to collect Rev.io invoices.
  • Billing and payments belong together.
  • From invoice to payment without the sync headache.
  • Lower payment fees where Rev.io can credibly prove savings.

Visual Direction

  • Before/after workflow: Rev.io invoice to QuickBooks payment vs. Rev.io invoice to Rev.io payment.
  • Calculator: payment rate delta, reconciliation time, sync breakage risk.

ICP And Value Planning

ICP Is A Fit System, Not A Persona

Planning should connect customer pain, Rev.io proof, implementation readiness, and economic outcome.

Win Now

$3M-$12M MSPs with fragmented billing, PSA, payment, and service workflows.

Win With Commitment

Accounts needing roadmap clarity, migration help, or partner integration proof.

Narrow

Smaller operators, weak urgency, low payment volume, or low operational complexity.

Stop

Deals requiring Rev.io to be positioned as native security/RMM platform.

RMM And Security Guardrails

Do Not Lead With Security. Lead With Service Monetization.

Working Copy

  • Rev.io is RMM/security agnostic.
  • Acronis: cloud backup first, then EDR, then RMM.
  • NinjaOne co-marketing supports RMM + Acronis backup/EDR + Rev.io service/billing workflows.
  • One bill, not three bills for three service types.

Visual Direction

  • Workflow diagram: RMM/security alert → ticket/service workflow → invoice/payment.
  • Label integrated/partner-powered capabilities clearly.
  • Avoid “Rev.io is a security platform.”
