10
ICP shift

Move upmarket around service providers with operational complexity

The ICP shift is about finding companies that feel the value of an integrated service operations + revenue management platform — not simply larger logos.

Sweet spot

MSP/CSP service providers

10+ employees$3M–$12M revenue

Fit signals

  • Complex recurring, usage, project, endpoint, communications, or AI service billing
  • Disconnected PSA, billing, payments, accounting, and customer workflows
  • Enough operational complexity to value platform consolidation

Disqualifiers

  • Very small operators with low complexity
  • Low payment volume or weak monetization urgency
  • Deals that need Rev.io positioned as a native RMM/security platform

Why it matters

The $1K minimum works when prospects see Rev.io as the revenue operating layer — not another PSA line item.

Pricing shift

The $1K minimum works if the value story moves beyond seats

The new pricing model should be positioned as a platform commitment, not a PSA-seat tax.

01

Pricing architecture

$1,000 minimum monthly commitment can be achieved through any combination of seats, Revii credits, Communications Billing, RMM, EDR, Backup, and payments.

02

Revii credits

Introduce AI credits for meaningful Revii usage; do not bill for basic commands. Position AI credits around work value, not nickel-and-dime consumption.

03

Sales message

Buyers are not paying for a product list. They are buying operational leverage: service delivery, billing, payment collection, customer operations, and AI-assisted execution.

04

Risk to avoid

Do not let competitors pull the conversation into seat-count pricing comparisons. Anchor to revenue capture, tool consolidation, implementation risk, and operational throughput.

Risks and dependencies

The plan fails if proof and source truth lag behind spend

These are the major risks leadership should address directly.

Attribution risk

If channel/source data stays messy, budget decisions will optimize toward visible activity instead of profitable pipeline.

Proof risk

If Product cannot provide approved proof, campaigns will overpromise or stay generic.

Pricing risk

If value story does not move beyond seats, $1K minimum creates friction instead of qualification.

Conversion risk

If no-shows, weak qualification, and feature-loss recovery are not fixed, more leads will not close the MRR gap.

Content risk

If blogs compete with money pages or competitor pages stay off-domain/underbuilt, SEO and paid will underperform.
