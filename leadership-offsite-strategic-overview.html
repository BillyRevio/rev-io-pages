08
ICP shift

Move upmarket around service providers with operational complexity

The ICP shift is about finding companies that feel the value of an integrated service operations + revenue management platform — not simply larger logos.

Sweet spot

MSP/CSP service providers

10+ employees$3M–$12M revenue

Fit signals

  • Complex recurring, usage, project, endpoint, communications, or AI service billing
  • Disconnected PSA, billing, payments, accounting, and customer workflows
  • Enough operational complexity to value platform consolidation

Disqualifiers

  • Very small operators with low complexity
  • Low payment volume or weak monetization urgency
  • Deals that need Rev.io positioned as a native RMM/security platform

Why it matters

The $1K minimum works when prospects see Rev.io as the service and revenue operating layer for their business — not another PSA line item.

Pricing shift

The $1K minimum works if the value story moves beyond seats

The new pricing model works best as a platform commitment, not a PSA-seat tax.

01

Pricing architecture

$1,000 minimum monthly commitment can be achieved through any combination of seats, Revii credits, Communications Billing, RMM, EDR, Backup, and payments.

02

Revii credits

Introduce AI credits for meaningful Revii usage; do not bill for basic commands. Position AI credits around work value, not nickel-and-dime consumption.

03

Sales message

Buyers are not paying for a product list. They are buying operational leverage: service delivery, billing, payment collection, customer operations, and AI-assisted execution.

04

Risk to avoid

Do not let competitors pull the conversation into seat-count pricing comparisons. Anchor to revenue capture, tool consolidation, implementation risk, and operational throughput.

Competitive Plays

Competitive win model

Win by forcing the buying criteria onto Rev.io’s terrain

The attack plan should make buyers evaluate competitors against Rev.io’s strongest advantage: service operations and revenue management in one operating layer.

1

Separate the arenas

Do not treat PSA and billing competitors as one list. PSA buyers care about service execution; billing buyers care about monetization complexity. The win story changes by arena.

2

Expose the tradeoff

Show what the buyer gives up with each competitor: disconnected billing, ecosystem lock-in, shallow monetization, weak payments, or limited AI-assisted workflow.

3

Prove the path

Lead with proof: migration plan, demos, workflow clips, billing scenarios, implementation timeline, security/trust material, and customer evidence.

4

Route to conversion

Each page and ad should move to a specific CTA: comparison guide, demo clip, product showcase, switching assessment, or competitor-specific sales sequence.

04

Sales motion

Battlecard, talk track, discovery questions, objection-specific nurture, closed-lost winback, and competitor-specific proof sent after discovery.

Risks and dependencies

The plan fails if proof and source truth lag behind spend

These are the major risks leadership needs to address directly.

Attribution risk

If channel/source data stays messy, budget decisions will optimize toward visible activity instead of profitable pipeline.

Proof risk

If Product cannot provide approved proof, campaigns will overpromise or stay generic.

Pricing risk

If value story does not move beyond seats, $1K minimum creates friction instead of qualification.

Conversion risk

If no-shows, weak qualification, and feature-loss recovery are not fixed, more leads will not close the MRR gap.

Content risk

If blogs compete with money pages or competitor pages stay off-domain/underbuilt, SEO and paid will underperform.

38
Leadership decisions

Decisions needed before H2 execution

H2 execution requires named decisions, not a long list of good ideas.

  • Do we align on service operations + revenue management as the category story?
  • Do we approve the $1K minimum pricing narrative and Revii credit framing?
  • Which competitive lane ships first, and which proof assets are required before spend scales? Recommended: PSA lane first (ConnectWise, HaloPSA, Autotask/Kaseya, SuperOps), billing lane next (OneBill, Datagate, Logisense).
  • Do we fund paid media at $35K/month only after conversion infrastructure is ready?
  • Do we commit to +15 web leads/month by EOY and the content/page roadmap required?
  • Do we commit to 2 Brook-led events/month plus 1 monthly product showcase?
