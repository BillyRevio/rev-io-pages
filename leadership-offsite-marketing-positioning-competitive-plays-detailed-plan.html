Executive thesis

H2 needs sharper conversion, not just more activity

H1 produced meaningful activity, but MRR conversion lagged. The answer is a tighter story, stronger proof, and focused competitive displacement.

Stop competing only as “PSA” or “billing software.” Own service operations + revenue management for modern MSPs.

06
Messaging guardrails

What we should and should not claim

Sharper messaging also means clearer boundaries — especially around AI, security/RMM, and pricing.

Use

AI-native for Revii/workflows; security/RMM-agnostic for partner-powered/integrated capabilities; billing and payments belong together.

Avoid

“Rev.io is a security platform,” generic all-in-one language, PSA-seat comparison traps, or low-price positioning.

Why it matters

Guardrails protect trust while making the platform story more believable.

17
Pricing + promotions

Use offers that reduce risk without cheapening the platform

If minimum pricing moves toward $575–$1K/month, Rev.io must be positioned around operational consolidation and revenue capture — not seat cost.

Pricing narrative

Reduced tool sprawl, less manual billing/reconciliation, better capture of billable work, fewer integrations, faster implementation, AI-assisted throughput.

Promotion principle

Reduce perceived switching risk; avoid discounting as the primary offer.

Avoid

Seat-price comparison traps, generic “free demo” CTAs, promotions that imply implementation is hard without concessions.

Leadership decisions

What needs to be decided before execution

The offsite should end with decisions that unlock work, owners, and deadlines.

  • Do we align on service operations + revenue management as the category direction?
  • Which headline promise leads: Bill for anything. Manage everything. / Service, accelerated / Your revenue engine for service operations?
  • Which three competitors get first campaign priority?
  • What payment savings claims are approved and provable?
  • Which customers can be approached for proof in the next 30 days?
  • Who owns customer advocacy and review generation?
  • What is Brook’s primary charter and cadence?
  • What campaign measurement standard determines scale or stop?
