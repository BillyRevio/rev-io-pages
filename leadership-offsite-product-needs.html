Product Need 01 · Demo environments

Build demo paths that let buyers experience value before Sales has to carry it

H2 conversion needs more than screenshots. We need interactive paths that support self-start evaluation, product-led proof, sales acceleration, and easier checkout.

Self-start trial path

  • Self-serve domain-based spin-up similar to HaloPSA trial expectations.
  • Pre-built products, workflows, tickets, invoices, quotes, and customer records so trial users see value immediately.
  • Use-case entry paths by MSP, CSP, and security integrator.
  • Trial setup data flows directly into onboarding and Sales follow-up.

Customer utilization metrics

  • Track feature usage, setup completion, active workflows, and usage depth.
  • Add in-application Revii credit consumption tracking, similar to Clay, so users understand usage and value in context.
  • Score trial/product engagement for sales acceleration campaigns.
  • Trigger outreach based on high-intent activity and stalled setup.
  • Use utilization data to personalize nurture and expansion plays.

Self checkout

  • Simple package selection for qualified starter motions.
  • Payment collection and account provisioning path.
  • Guardrails for minimum commitment, add-ons, and assisted-buying thresholds.
  • Route larger/complex buyers to Sales without killing momentum.

Demo asset library

  • Recorded demos by workflow and feature.
  • Short clips for competitor pages and nurture.
  • Sales-ready walkthroughs for quote-to-cash, dispatch-to-invoice, payments, and Revii.
  • Product-approved screenshots that stay current.

Product Need 02 · Quote-to-bill foundation

Fix the quote-to-bill gaps

The common thread is quote-to-bill trust: customers need confidence that what gets sold, ordered, provisioned, and billed can move through Rev.io without manual back-office work or fragile white-label experiences.

Internal client billing

  • Continues to be a major operational challenge and creates concerning customer optics.
  • Multiple products are sold today but still require manual behind-the-scenes billing work.
  • Need a cleaner operating model for product setup, billing rules, invoice readiness, and accountability.

Orders as the bridge

  • Legacy billing customers have not migrated because orders are not available.
  • The core function is bridging the gap between a service being sold and being ready to bill.
  • Orders should connect quote, product/catalog, provisioning status, billing activation, and downstream invoice accuracy.

Native quoting decision

  • Concern: white-labeling PandaDoc may not solve the native quote-to-cash workflow problem.
  • Evaluate native quoting / CPQ path versus a direct PandaDoc integration.
  • Decision should be based on control of catalog, approvals, orders, contracts, and billing handoff.

GTM impact

  • These gaps affect migration confidence, billing credibility, and platform completeness.
  • Sales needs approved language for what is native, what is integrated, and what is roadmap.
  • Marketing should not over-claim quote-to-bill until Product confirms the path.

Product Need 03 · Commercial controls

Build native commercial controls

The quote-to-bill path needs more than document generation. Customers need governed CPQ, promotions, progressive billing, and GL allocation that flow through quote, order, invoice, accounting, and reporting workflows.

CPQ, discounting & promotions

  • Time-limited discounts need to appear correctly on quotes and bills.
  • Promo code support is missing for campaign-driven and contract-specific offers.
  • True CPQ capability is needed for packaging, approvals, discount rules, and clean quote-to-order handoff.

Progressive / installment billing

  • Customers want to split large project invoices across multiple months or invoices.
  • Scheduling should be automated rather than manually tracked by billing teams.
  • Progress billing should connect project milestones, quote/order terms, invoice timing, and revenue visibility.

GL rules / allocation

  • Rules-based GL cost allocation is a top customer request distinct from SKU-level GL codes.
  • Customers need bucketing by reason codes and configurable accounting logic.
  • Allocation rules should support finance workflows without forcing manual cleanup outside Rev.io.

Profitability everywhere

  • Profitability should be visible across customer, project, ticket, product, contract, and invoice views.
  • Companies track profitability differently, so configurability is required.
  • This ties back to early PRFAQ thinking: make margin impact visible inside the operating workflow.

Product Need 04 · Scale + service operations

Prove the platform can scale

These needs are conversion and retention signals: they affect whether larger prospects trust the platform, whether complex service teams can operate inside it, and whether Rev.io can separate from competitors in sales conversations.

System performance

  • Page load times need to improve before larger clients add more platform strain.
  • Performance should be treated as buyer trust, not just technical debt.
  • GTM needs credible language around scalability, responsiveness, and larger-account readiness.

Limited-access non-employee users

  • Subcontractor and co-managed use cases require limited-access user types for non-employees.
  • Permission slices should differ by role: project, ticket, customer, schedule, inventory, or billing visibility.
  • This supports co-managed service delivery without overexposing customer or financial data.

AI ticket routing

  • Consistently comes up in sales presentations as a desirable future capability.
  • Opportunity to separate from competitors by showing what is possible in-market and where Revii can go next.
  • Needs clear roadmap status: available, planned, partner-led, or future vision.

Non-stock inventory

  • Not currently on the roadmap, but flagged as a sales blocker.
  • Need a Product decision on whether to roadmap, partner/integrate, position as workaround, or explicitly exclude.
  • Sales and Marketing need approved language so this does not become a late-stage surprise.

Product Need 05 · Security + trust

Package the security and trust proof buyers already ask for

We have the foundation. H2 needs it organized into buyer-facing and sales-ready proof that lowers switching risk for mature providers.

Security proof

  • Architecture and data-protection overview.
  • Revii AI security protocol language.
  • Integration standards and permission model.
  • Trust/compliance claims approved for public use.

Switching-risk proof

  • Implementation timeline and migration checklist.
  • Billing continuity explanation.
  • Onboarding path by customer type.
  • Customer references for larger operators.

Sales enablement

  • Security FAQ for late-stage deals.
  • Buyer-ready one-pager for IT/security review.
  • Objection handling for AI, data, migration, and integrations.
  • Approved language for competitor campaigns.

Website + campaign assets

  • Security/trust page module.
  • Downloadable migration guide.
  • Trust proof embedded on competitor pages.
  • Proof blocks for paid retargeting and nurture.

Product Need 06 · GTM/Product communication alignment

Marketing and Sales need roadmap visibility before features release

Product communication should create market readiness before launch, not after. Each meaningful release needs a GTM packet that Sales, Marketing, and CS can use immediately.

Roadmap visibility

  • Forward-looking visibility into features with GTM impact.
  • Public, sales-only, and internal-only claim guidance.
  • Competitive neutralizers before prospects raise the objection.
  • Known limitations and workaround language.

Sales training

  • Pre-release enablement for high-impact features.
  • Discovery questions and qualification fit.
  • Objection handling by competitor and product gap.
  • Battlecard updates tied to release milestones.

Recorded demos ahead of release

  • Short internal demo clips before launch.
  • Buyer-facing clips once claims are approved.
  • Workflow demos mapped to closed-lost reasons.
  • Feature proof reusable across web, nurture, paid, and sales follow-up.

Launch handoff

  • Release note → sales talk track → campaign asset workflow.
  • Owner for screenshots, demos, FAQs, and roadmap language.
  • Winback trigger list for opportunities that requested the feature.
  • Feedback loop from Sales objections back to Product.
